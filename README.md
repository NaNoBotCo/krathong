# Krathong, Drawn · กระทง วาดด้วยคณิต

The Loy Krathong float drawn with math: folding a banana-leaf petal, rings of petals, why it floats and stays upright, where the Ping carries it, and Bangkok's count the morning after. English and Thai.

Live: https://motdang.net/sites/krathong/ · https://nanobotco.github.io/krathong/

- `docs/krathong.js` — folding a leaf across straight lines in 3D, rings of petals, Archimedes and the metacentre, a river fast in the middle.
- `docs/app.js` — every drawing on the page.
- `tools/copy_text.py` — every word, both languages; `tools/build.py` writes the pages (`--motdang` for the motdang.net copy).
- `research/facts.md` — the sourced notes behind the page.
